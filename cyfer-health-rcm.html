Billing infrastructure,
not just billing staff.

Cyfer Health RCM is Cyfer Solutions' medical billing and revenue cycle management division, built for clinics, practices, and providers who want their billing handled from patient intake through final payment without expanding internal headcount.

We pair certified billing expertise with the systems discipline of a software company: cleaner data, fewer errors, and real visibility into where your revenue actually stands.
